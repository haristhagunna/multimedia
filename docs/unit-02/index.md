# Unit 2: Sound and Audio

**4 hours · weeks 2–3 · syllabus items 2.1 (sound and music) and 2.2 (speech)**

Unit 1 told you *what* multimedia is. This unit starts the tour of the individual media with the one you can hear: **sound**. You will learn what sound physically is, how a computer turns it into numbers, how music is described as instructions (**MIDI**) instead of samples, and how computers **generate**, **analyse** and **transmit** human speech.

[Open the class slides](slides.md){ .md-button .md-button--primary }

## What you must master

1. **Define** sound, waveform, period, frequency and amplitude, and give the frequency ranges.
2. **Distinguish** sound from audio.
3. **Explain** how an analog sound wave becomes digital: **sampling**, the **Nyquist theorem** and **quantization**, and **calculate file size** with the formula.
4. **Describe MIDI**: the two parts of the interface, messages, devices, channels, and the **MIDI versus SMPTE** timing difference.
5. **Explain speech generation** (time-dependent and frequency-dependent concatenation, formants) and the **two steps of speech synthesis**.
6. **Explain speech analysis** (who, what, how) and **speech recognition**.
7. **Explain speech transmission** (PCM, source encoding, recognition/synthesis) and compare the common **audio file formats**.

## Suggested last-week routine

1. One unit per day: read the overview and the **Exam focus** boxes only.
2. Attempt the practice questions *before* revealing answers; redo missed ones with flashcards.
3. Recompute every numerical on the formula sheet without looking.
4. Final day: glossary, checklist, then rest.

## Formulas to memorise

| Quantity | Formula |
|---|---|
| Effective vertical resolution | active scan lines × Kell factor (0.7) |
| Required horizontal resolution | aspect ratio × effective vertical resolution |
| Video bandwidth | a × b × c / 2 |
| Luminance | Y = 0.30R + 0.59G + 0.11B |
| Raw frame size | width × height × bits per pixel |
| Raw data rate | frame size × frame rate |

!!! exam "Where marks come from"
    Expect one numerical question (raw video size, bandwidth or Kell factor), one on RGB/YUV/YIQ/composite, one on flicker and continuity of motion, and one on animation control or display (double buffering, symbolic vs pixmap).

## Using the site

- Mark pages complete to track progress (stored only in your own browser).
- Practice pages offer tap-to-answer MCQs, expand/collapse all and flashcards.
- Use the [revision planner](revision/planner.md) to turn your exam date into a day-by-day plan.
- Press `/` or `s` to search. Press the moon/sun icon to switch theme.

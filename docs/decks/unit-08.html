QoS and resource management span all layers

Application subsystem

  • CSCW: video conference, whiteboard, shared applications
  • Session management: setup, membership, media, floor control
  • Centralised (MCU) or distributed
  • Multimedia mail (MIME)

Transport requirements

  • High throughput · bounded delay · small jitter
  • QoS support · multicast
  • Timeliness over perfect reliability
  • Synchronisation · low overhead

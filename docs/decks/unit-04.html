Unit 4

Video and Animation

Multimedia and Application (BCA429CO)

What is video?

  • Image + audio; a sequence of frames
  • Frame rate in fps: 20 to 30 looks smooth
  • Persistence of vision merges the frames
  • Three aspects: visual representation, transmission, digitization

Visual representation: the measures

  • Vertical detail and viewing distance
  • Horizontal detail and picture width
  • Total detail content · Depth perception
  • Luminance and chrominance
  • Temporal aspects of illumination
  • Continuity of motion · Flicker · Temporal aspect of bandwidth

Kell factor

effective vertical = active lines × 0.7


horizontal = aspect ratio × effective vertical

PAL: 576 × 0.7 = 403.2; 4/3 × 403.2 = 537.6

Cures: raise refresh rate, or interlaced scanning (odd lines, then even lines)

Video bandwidth

B = a × b × c / 2

a = pixels per line, b = lines, c = frame rate

1312 × 800 × 100 / 2 = 52.48 MHz

Digitization and computer video

  • Sampling: spatial position (M × N points)
  • Quantizing: colour / grey levels
  • Standard: CCIR-601 (ITU-R)
  • Raster: display controller → refresh buffer → video controller → monitor
  • CLUT: pixel value = address of colour

Raw video size

frame = W × H × bits · rate = frame × fps

Display and transmission

  • ≥ 10 (prefer 15–20) scan conversions/s → ≤ 100 ms per image
  • Double buffering: display one, build the other
  • Symbolic: fast to send, slower to display
  • Pixmap: slower to send, fast to display

Multimedia System

Multimedia and Application (BCA429CO)

What is multimedia?

  • Medium: means for distribution and presentation of information
  • Multimedia: computer-controlled integration of text, graphics, images, audio, video, animation
  • Represented, stored, transmitted and processed digitally
  • Delivered interactively

Building blocks

ElementKey idea
TextHTML began as simple text display
AudioVibration heard by the ear: speech, effects, music
Graphics2D figures, drawn by hand or computer
VideoCapture, record, process, transmit moving pictures
AnimationRapid image sequence: persistence of vision

History

  • 1966: Bob Goldstein coins "multimedia"
  • 1972: Atari's Pong, graphics + sound
  • 1977: Apple II (colour graphics)
  • 1981 / 1984: IBM PC / Macintosh with GUI and mouse
  • 1990s: "multimedia" the overused term
  • 2000s on: streaming, VR, AR

Aspects of multimedia

  • Interactivity
  • Integration
  • Hyperlinking
  • User control
  • Multisensory experience

Linear vs non-linear

  • Linear: fixed order (book, film)
  • Non-linear: user controls content (games, interactive video)
  • Hypertext: read by following links
  • Hypermedia: links to any media; the WWW

Media concepts

  • Representation space: visual (paper, screen), acoustic (mono, stereo)
  • Representation values: continuous or discrete
  • Representation dimensions: spatial (2D, 3D) and temporal

Discrete = time-independent (document) · Continuous = time-dependent (movie)

Properties of a multimedia system

  • Combination of media: needs discrete and continuous
  • Independence: several levels
  • Computer-supported integration: programmable control

Text + pictures alone is not multimedia.

Challenges

  • Synchronization and sequencing
  • Latency and delay on networks
  • Large storage, very high bandwidth
  • Complex algorithms and hardware
  • Analysis and indexing harder than text

# Video Disks: Laser Vision, DVD and Blu-ray

!!! source "Source"
    *Unit 6 – cd* pp. 10–11 (Laser Vision), pp. 49–52 (DVD). **Blu-ray is not in the lecturer's notes.**

## Laser Vision

| Feature | Value |
|---|---|
| Stores | **Video and audio** |
| Encoding | **Analogue** |
| Quality | High quality of reproduced data |
| Diameter | about **30 cm** |
| Capacity | about **2.6 GB** |
| Origin | Video Long Play (VLP), described in 1973 |

Principles: mix of audio and video, **frequency modulation**, and **no quantization of pit length** (pit length is analogue).

## DVD: Digital Video (Versatile) Disk

- Goal: store an **entire high-quality digital movie on a single side** of a disc.
- Same size as CD, so drives stay **backward compatible**; higher capacity from **shorter pits and lands** and **tighter tracks**; **EFM Plus** error correction, more robust than the CD scheme.
- Formats: **single-sided single-layer**; **single-sided double-layer** (laser switches focus); **double-sided** (disc must be flipped).

| | CD | DVD |
|---|---|---|
| Diameter / thickness | 120 mm / 1.2 mm | 120 mm / 1.2 mm |
| Laser wavelength | **780 nm** (infrared) | **650 and 635 nm** (red) |
| Track pitch | **1.6 µm** | **0.74 µm** |
| Min. pit/land length | **0.83 µm** | **0.4 µm** |
| Layers / sides | 1 / 1 | 1 or 2 / 1 or 2 |
| Capacity | about **650 MB** | **4.7 GB** single layer, **8.5 GB** dual layer, **9.4 GB** double-sided |
| Video data rate | about 1.5 Mbit/s | 1–10 Mbit/s variable |
| Video compression | **MPEG-1** | **MPEG-2** |
| Video capacity | about 1 hour | more than 4 hours (dual layer) |
| Sound | 2-channel MPEG | 2-channel PCM, 5.1-channel AC-3, up to 8 streams |
| Subtitles | – | up to 32 languages |

!!! warning "Slip in the lecturer's slide"
    The DVD wavelength is printed as "650 and 635 **mm**"; it is **nm**.

**DVD variants:** **DVD-ROM** (data), **DVD-Video** (movies), **DVD-Audio**, **DVD-R** (record once), **DVD-RAM** (erasable, rewritable). **Devices:** DVD movie player (consumer), DVD-ROM drive (computer).

!!! example "Capacity gain"
    4.7 GB ÷ 650 MB ≈ **7.2 times** a CD (4,700 ÷ 650).

## Blu-ray disc

!!! info "Not in the previous lecturer's notes"
    Written from standard textbook knowledge, to complete syllabus item 6.2. Check the figures against your prescribed textbook.

- A third generation of the same idea: **shorter wavelength** and **tighter tracks**.
- **Blue-violet laser, 405 nm** (hence the name); **numerical aperture 0.85**; track pitch **0.32 µm**.
- Capacity **25 GB** single layer, **50 GB** dual layer, same 120 mm size, so players are backward compatible with CD and DVD.
- Thin **0.1 mm cover layer** over the data, so the lens can be very close.
- Stores **high-definition (HD) video** with modern codecs (MPEG-2, H.264, VC-1). Variants: **BD-ROM, BD-R, BD-RE**.

| | CD | DVD | Blu-ray |
|---|---|---|---|
| Laser | 780 nm infrared | 650 nm red | **405 nm** blue-violet |
| Track pitch | 1.6 µm | 0.74 µm | **0.32 µm** |
| Capacity (1 layer) | 650 MB | 4.7 GB | **25 GB** |

!!! exam "Exam tip"
    "Differentiate CD and DVD" is a table question: copy the table above. Add one line that DVD gains capacity from a **shorter wavelength, smaller pits, tighter tracks and two layers**.

## Checkpoint

??? question "Why is Laser Vision called analogue?"
    Video and audio are frequency modulated and pit lengths are not quantized.

??? question "List three reasons DVD holds more than CD."
    Red laser with shorter wavelength, track pitch 0.74 µm instead of 1.6 µm, shorter pits (0.4 µm), plus dual layers.

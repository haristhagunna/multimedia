## Short answer

??? question "1. List the classes of multimedia applications. (4 marks)"
    Game systems, multimedia repositories, interactive TV and video on demand, video/phone conferencing and hypermedia mail, shared workspaces and executive environments, business process workflow applications.

??? question "2. Why were game systems the leaders in multimedia? (2 marks)"
    The market is very large and quality demands, though intense, are not crucial to success.

??? question "3. What is a set-top box? (2 marks)"
    The next-generation digital system connecting the digital network to the TV and home appliances, providing interactive services.

??? question "4. Differentiate shared workspace and shared executive environment. (3 marks)"
    Output of one application shown remotely vs the same application run locally on each workstation with the same data.

??? question "5. Differentiate dedicated, departmental and enterprise-wide systems. (3 marks)"
    Self-contained; LAN-based for a department; many interconnected LANs/WANs.

??? question "6. Name the multimedia storage servers. (3 marks)"
    Database, image, voice mail, audio, video servers, duplication station.

??? question "7. What is a storyboard? (3 marks)"
    A detailed description by words and sketches of every screen, sound and navigation choice, drawn on paper or with a tool.

??? question "8. Name four architecture types. (2 marks)"
    Linear, hierarchical, non-linear, composite.

??? question "9. Differentiate alpha and beta testing. (3 marks)"
    Internal vs wider group of representative users.

??? question "10. List the items to include in a delivered application. (3 marks)"
    Media elements, runtime libraries, drivers, helper programs, installation and compression/decompression programs.

??? question "11. Write short notes on video on demand. (5 marks)  *(textbook)*"
    Definition, architecture (server, network, set-top box), true vs near VoD, requirements.

??? question "12. Write short notes on video conferencing. (5 marks)  *(textbook)*"
    Point-to-point and multipoint, MCU, shared whiteboard, requirements (delay ≤ 150 ms, lip sync ±80 ms), H.320/H.323.

??? question "13. Write short notes on digital libraries. (5 marks)  *(textbook)*"
    Digital collections with metadata, indexing and retrieval, preservation, access control, networked access.

## Long answer

??? question "14. Explain the multimedia application development life cycle. (8 marks)"
    Five stages: planning and costing (hardware, software, contents, skills), designing (storyboard, architecture, user interface), developing and producing (rights and permissions), testing (alpha and beta), delivering.

??? question "15. Explain the components of a multimedia system. (8 marks)"
    Input, output and storage systems with their nodes and servers.

??? question "16. Discuss the user interface design of a multimedia application with the surveillance example. (6 marks)"
    Content selection constraints, adequate media; keyboard, buttons, scroll bars, joystick, direct manipulation; user-friendliness goals.

??? question "17. Explain copyright issues in multimedia development. (5 marks)"
    Nine protected work types; license before use; questions on use, period, exclusivity, region, authority, remuneration.

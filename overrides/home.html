How to study from this site

  1. Skim the unit overview and the class slides for the big picture.
  2. Read the topic notes in order, answering each checkpoint first.
  3. Practise with the MCQs and flashcards, then write long answers on paper.
  4. Revise from the Formula sheet, Glossary and Exam checklist before the paper.
